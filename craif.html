06 / DATA SPECIALIST

Exploring medical images with AI-assisted analysis.

Craif Inc. · Data Specialist · March – June 2025 · Finland

Project context

Image analysis can help quantify structures that are difficult to describe consistently by eye — but research results need careful validation.

My work at Craif involved AI-related medical imaging and research pipelines.

The work included X-ray image-analysis workflows, tumour segmentation, size and shape analysis, and 2D-to-3D reconstruction and visualisation. It also involved research into generative neural networks for predicting tumour growth or metastasis.

The focus was on research and technical data workflows rather than presenting a clinical diagnostic product. Medical use requires appropriate validation, clinical oversight and regulatory compliance.
