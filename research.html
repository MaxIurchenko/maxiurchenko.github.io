MASTER’S THESIS

Hyperspectral imaging for metal alloy identification.

University of Eastern Finland · 2023–2025

Can spectral signatures and neural networks help distinguish metal alloys?

My Master’s thesis explored the use of hyperspectral imaging and neural networks for metal-alloy identification. The work involved preparing and inspecting spectral data, developing preprocessing and analysis workflows, and evaluating classification approaches.

The project connects an industrial need with computational analysis: a metal-processing environment provides the problem, while spectroscopy and machine learning offer methods to investigate it.
