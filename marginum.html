PORTFOLIO / EXPERIENCE / MARGINUM

Finding meaningful patterns in noisy signals.

Marginum · Data Specialist · October 2023 – October 2024 · Finland

Project context

Signal data is rarely perfect. Useful analysis begins with understanding the noise, the expected events and the limits of the measurements.

At Marginum, my work focused on signal-processing workflows for peak detection and classification.

The work involved noisy sensor and modem data, where relevant signal events need to be separated from background variation. Related topics included compression and storage optimisation, as well as embedded and telecommunications architectures.

This experience developed my interest in data-processing pipelines that can be inspected, tested and adapted to the constraints of the system generating the data.
