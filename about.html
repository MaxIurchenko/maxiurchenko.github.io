01 / WHO I AM

An engineer who connects hands-on production with software and research.

My background combines CNC manufacturing, automation, software development and computer science research.

How I approach technical work

Understand the process. Find the signal. Build something that works in the real world.

I am interested in problems where physical systems and digital tools meet.

Manufacturing taught me to respect real-world constraints: machine behaviour, tooling, repeatability, setup time and quality. Software development taught me to break problems into components and build reusable solutions. Research gave me experience with experimental data, analytical methods and evaluating models rather than assuming they work.

Today, I want to keep developing at the intersection of precision engineering, automation, research software and applied AI.
